DELHI POLICE CRIME BRANCH SUMMARY
Date: 10 March 2026
FIR: 58/2026 PS Vasant Kunj South

During inspection of the logistics depot located at NH8 Mahipalpur Warehouse, officers detained fleet supervisor Manoj Tiwari. The premises are registered under Cityline Auto Rentals and leased to Vikram Malhotra, Managing Director of Apex Global Logistics Pvt Ltd. 

Scrutiny of gate registers revealed that vehicle UP14 AB 1234 (registered to Rahul Kumar) and vehicle DL01 CA 8899 (registered to Vikram Malhotra) made frequent simultaneous entries between 22:00 and 03:00 hours. A black briefcase containing financial ledgers linked to Sunita Rao of Kuber Financial Services was seized from the office desk.
